# myChess version history

Compact, chronological summary of myChess releases — what changed, the measured Elo delta where available, and the rough CCRL-Blitz strength estimate at each release.

Coverage starts at **v3.0.0** (May 2026), when the two earlier engine variants (`engines/v1`, `engines/v2`) were removed and `MyChessEngine` became the sole engine. The 2019/2020 v1.0.0 line in git history belongs to that earlier, now-deleted lineage.

For the complementary *search-behavior* view of the same releases — the `bench`
node signature per version, which characterizes what kind of change a release
was rather than how strong it made the engine — see the
[bench history](bench-history.md).

## Notes on the estimated CCRL Blitz column

- The **anchor** is the test12 measurement of myChess 3.1.x against Pulse 1.7.3 (CCRL Blitz = 1505 as of 2026-05): myChess scored Pulse − 64 ± 31 Elo at TC 40/120 over 400 games. That places 3.1.x at **~1441 CCRL Blitz**, with ±31 Elo absolute uncertainty.
- Versions 3.2.0–3.5.2 carry the anchor forward because every intervening change was either a non-strength variant (Chess960), a neutral-by-design refactor (fail-soft), or an investigation whose own measurement was noise / null (doubled-pawn −5.6 ± 21, connection-quality null). Net assumption: **3.1.x ≈ 3.5.2 ± 15 Elo**.
- 3.6.0 = 3.5.2 + 28.1. 4.0.0 = 3.6.0 + 92.7. 4.0.1 = 4.0.0 + 1.1 (measured null, kept in master because the larger default is harmless and may help at longer TCs). 4.0.2 = 4.0.1 + 9.3 (equivalent to 4.0.0 + 9.3 since 4.0.1 was null). 4.0.3 = 4.0.2 + 15.6. All five deltas were measured at TC 40/60 SPRT / fixed-N matches against the immediately-prior version, so they propagate cleanly.
- The 4.0.1 null result corrects an over-confident pre-test prediction of +10-15 Elo from the TT-size enlargement; see [roadmap § 12.1 follow-up](roadmap-done.md#follow-up-4-tt-default-size-in-v401--null-effect-at-tc-4060) for the postmortem (depth-preferred-EXACT replacement is more effective than eviction-rate alone implied; saturation point at TC 40/60 was already at ~1 M, not at ~4-16 M).
- The 4.0.2 +9.3 Elo point estimate is nominally sub-95 %-LOS on its own SPRT (LLR 1.50, LOS 90.5 %), but the effect is confirmed in aggregate: six independent depth-aware bucket variants all landed in the +8-12 Elo band vs the same v4.0.1 baseline. The choice of the simplest such variant (`tt-bucket-depth`) is documented in [roadmap § 12.1 follow-up](roadmap-done.md#follow-up-tt-bucket-replacement-strategies--explored-depth-only-chosen); a re-evaluation of the more complex `admission-hitcount` variant is planned for the post-NMP/LMR/QSearch world.
- The 4.0.3 +15.6 Elo effect was larger than the pre-test "storage-refactor-neutral" prediction. Two plausible contributors: (a) cache locality — 4-slot bucket occupies ~96 bytes contiguous off-heap (~1.5 cache lines) vs `TTEntry[]` where the four referenced objects may be scattered across the heap (~5-8 cache lines worst case per bucket scan); (b) reduced Serial-GC card-table work — the old generation no longer contains 4 M `TTEntry` reference cells that must be scanned on every minor collection.
- The **4.0.4–4.0.7** versions carry the 4.0.3 estimate forward unchanged — none was measured; they are correctness fixes and tooling. **4.0.7 is the exception worth flagging.** It fixes a *latent* en-passant Zobrist-hash-drift bug that had been present throughout the entire v4.0.x TT line: only `_makeNormalMove` cleared the previous ply's en-passant hash contribution, so any pawn double-step followed by a promotion, castling, or en-passant capture left the incremental hash diverging from a from-scratch recomputation. A drifted position hash produces **false transposition-table hits** — cached scores and best-moves from unrelated positions — which means every prior TT Elo measurement (4.0.0–4.0.3) was taken with this noise/bias source silently active. The fix's own effect was not isolated (it merged as a correctness fix, no SPRT), but it removes an unquantified error term from all earlier TT deltas; the true TT contribution may be marginally larger than the propagated numbers suggest. The bug was surfaced indirectly during NMP bring-up via a per-node hash-consistency debug assertion; see [`PositionHashConsistencyRegressionTest`](../src/test/java/org/michaelfl/mychess/PositionHashConsistencyRegressionTest.java) for the regression coverage that now guards it.
- **4.1.0 = 4.0.7 + 76.0.** Null-move pruning — the second-largest measured single-feature jump after the TT (+92.7). Measured directly against v4.0.7, both sides carrying the en-passant Zobrist fix, so the delta is a clean NMP effect and not confounded by the 4.0.7 correctness fix. Propagates cleanly onto the ~1587 carried baseline → **~1663**. The gain is W/B-asymmetric (~+87 White vs ~+65 Black, ≈1.5σ at 1600 games/color), matching the directional pattern the TT showed — see [roadmap § 12.14](roadmap-done.md#1214-color-asymmetry-investigate-the-wb-bias-seen-in-cross-version-matches--s-evidence-weakening). As with every prior delta this is a *relative* measurement; the absolute ~1663 inherits the same propagated ±40 uncertainty discussed below.
- **4.2.0 = 4.1.1 + 60.4** (and 4.1.1 = 4.1.0, behavior-neutral). The all-captures quiescence upgrade — the largest single-feature jump since NMP (+76) and the TT (+92.7). Unlike the two shelved NMP refinements (aggressive R + verification search, +4.8, not significant), this one is clearly significant *and* **color-robust** (~+70 White / ~+51 Black, both well clear of zero). Propagates onto the ~1663 baseline → **~1723**. Only §12.6.1 (enter-at-every-leaf + all-captures) shipped in 4.2.0; the SEE / delta-pruning / TT-in-QSearch refinements (§§ 12.6.2–12.6.5) followed separately.
- **4.2.1 = 4.2.0 + 40.6.** SEE-based capture ordering + SEE < 0 pruning inside the QSearch (§§ 12.6.2–12.6.3, shipped as one bundle). The third-largest single-feature jump on the QSearch path, on the same footing as the all-captures upgrade — clearly significant (LOS 100 %) and **color-robust** (winning with both colors, 0.580 White / 0.537 Black). Measured directly against v4.2.0, so the delta is a clean SEE effect. Ordering and pruning were not A/B-split, so the ~+40.6 is their combined contribution. Propagates onto the ~1723 baseline → **~1764**. Of the QSearch sub-items only §12.6.4 (delta pruning) now remains open (§12.6.5 TT-in-QSearch was tried and shelved).
- **4.2.2 = 4.2.1** for playing strength. Offline Texel tuner + PGN-import robustness + the `bench` node-signature command — infrastructure for the upcoming tapered-eval tuning, none of which changes runtime search or eval. Carries ~1764 forward.
- **4.2.3 = 4.2.2 + 8.7** (≡ 4.2.1 + 8.7, since 4.2.2 is strength-neutral). A pure search-correctness fix (NMP PV-table stride), so the primary result is the elimination of the illegal-PV / assertion family — **6290 → 0** occurrences of *"First move must be the best known move"* across a 1600-game self-play match (~6 corruptions/game in v4.2.2, all silently recovered from — games completed, no forfeits — which is why the strength cost was modest). The SPRT ran to its 1600-game cap without crossing a bound (LLR 1.06, LOS 90.1 %) — the expected outcome for a small-but-real gain sitting between elo0 = −3 and elo1 = 15. The +8.7 point estimate is propagated on the same sub-95 %-LOS convention used for 4.0.2 (+9.3, LOS 90.5 %). Propagates onto ~1764 → **~1773**.
- **4.3.0 = 4.2.3 + 22.3.** The first tapered-evaluation payoff — landed once *both* long-standing prerequisites were finally in place: the `byte→short` PST-storage widening (so tuned values > 127 no longer wrap/sign-flip; § 12.7.1) and the MG/EG phase split. A phase-aware, **endgame-only** pawn-PST Texel tune (Zurichess quiet-labeled, midgame table fixed) produced advanced-pawn endgame values (rank 7 ≈ +145 cp) that measurably help; the earlier −15.6 eval-tuning-v1 inversion was driven by the missing prerequisites, not by "proxy ≠ Elo" being fatal. Clearly significant (LOS 98.7 %, SPRT H1 at 766 games) and color-robust. Propagates onto ~1773 → **~1795**. Next tapered target: the king MG/EG table (king-safety-lite in the midgame, endgame centralization in the endgame).
- **4.3.1 = 4.3.0 + 7.7.** The second tapered-evaluation piece — the king endgame table. The crude endgame king-PST skip (`plyCount > 60`, which simply *dropped* the king PST once past move 60) is replaced by a phase-blended, Texel-tuned king **endgame** table rewarding centralization (center up, back-rank-center ≈ −135); the midgame king table is held fixed. The SPRT vs 4.3.0 (band elo0 = −3 / elo1 = 15) converged to a small positive and was **stopped early**: the effect sits just above the midpoint (6), so the LLR only crept (0.42 / 2.94 ≈ 14 % at 990 games) and a formal H1 verdict would need ~7000 games — not worth it for a stable, theoretically-sound small gain. Propagated on the same sub-95 %-LOS convention as 4.0.2 (+9.3) and 4.2.3 (+8.7). Onto ~1795 → **~1803**. Next tapered step: a **joint endgame tune** of the remaining pieces (knight / bishop / rook / queen) in one SPRT — a larger combined effect that a SPRT can resolve decisively.
- **4.3.2 = 4.3.1 + 12.6.** That predicted joint endgame-PST tune came back **neutral** (~+3 Elo, LOS ~64 %) and was shelved — the mobile pieces' endgame *placement* is largely search/mobility-redundant. But its dominant raw signal was a phase-dependent *material* re-rating (queen up, knight down) that a placement table cannot express. Modelling that cleanly as tapered endgame material wanted the queen ~+72 cp higher in the endgame, which was diagnosed as a symptom of the *midgame* queen being undervalued (queen/rook ratio 1.8 vs "2R = Q" 2.0 / PeSTO ~2.15): the endgame-only tuner pushed the queen up where it could reach. **Raising the midgame queen value 900 → 1000 directly** (queen/rook → 2.0) is the fundamental, larger fix — measured **+12.6 ± 13.3, LOS 96.8 %** (the strongest-confirmed gain of the series, ran to the 1600-game cap at LLR 2.57), and it also resolved the Chess960 passive-rook weakness. Propagates onto ~1803 → **~1816**. The tapered-endgame-material and joint endgame-PST branches are shelved as redundant with this — all three were re-fixing the same undervalued queen.
- **4.3.3 = 4.3.2 + 31.3.** A **bishop-pair bonus** — a side with both bishops scores +0.4 pawns (~40 cp). myChess had no material-combination terms (knight = bishop = 300 flat), yet the bishop pair is the most reliable such bonus in computer chess (two bishops cover both color complexes and dominate open positions). It came from the same line of questioning as the queen fix — "do engines value holding both bishops / both knights higher?" (yes for bishops, no for knights — knights don't complement each other, and KNNvK is a draw). Implemented as the 8th tunable Texel factor so the 0.4 value can be tuned later, without changing the eval value. Measured **+31.3 ± 24.1, LOS 99.4 %, SPRT H1 accepted at 512 games** — the first formal H1 acceptance of the series and its largest single gain, a reminder that a missing *well-established* term can outweigh a lot of fine placement tuning (contrast the neutral joint endgame-PST). Propagates onto ~1816 → **~1847**.
- **4.3.4 = 4.3.3 + 23.** The **full-joint MG+EG PST tune** — the last planned lever of the tapered-PST/material series. All twelve tables were retuned jointly on a Zurichess + myChess **Chess960 self-play hybrid** (the self-play data track finally used, ~4 % of the set, two-thirds of it 960); the four mobile pieces get their first genuine midgame/endgame split (they previously shared one phase-blind table), and material + non-PST terms stayed fixed with every table re-centered to strip the material-leak component. The early SPRT hit +69, but that was **winner's-curse-inflated** (a favorable early stop); the unbiased **fixed-N settled at +23.0 ± 12.9, LOS 100 %** over 1900 games, color-robust — the honest number. Two isolated curated-position regressions (a knife-edge Sicilian queen retreat and a 960 knight retreat, both horizon-limited at depth 8) were noted in-test and outweighed by the net gain. Propagates onto ~1847 → **~1870**.

- **4.4.0 = 4.3.4 + 32.6.** The **PeSTO piece-square tables**, adopted wholesale — the first third-party material in the engine (credited in the class header, the [README](../README.md#credits-and-third-party-material), and [§ 12.7.5](roadmap.md#1275-pesto-piece-square-tables--done-326-elo-v440)). The diff is exactly one file: material and every other evaluation term are untouched, so the delta is attributable to placement values alone. Measured **+32.6 ± 12.4 over 2000 fixed-N games (LOS 100 %)** after an SPRT accepted H1 at +39.8 — quote it as **≈ +30**, since the closing rating-interval sample sat at the high end of a +28.3…+32.9 range. The result also **overturns the earlier "PST lever is exhausted" conclusion** drawn from the ≈ 0 PeSTO ceiling test: that null was better tables *minus* the removed myChess terms, two effects cancelling. Keeping both separates them — the tables are worth ≈ +33 over our Texel-tuned ones, our non-table terms ≈ +36 on top of PeSTO's. Methodological lesson: a null result from changing two things at once says nothing about either. Colors split unevenly (0.582 White / 0.511 Black, ~2σ), noted but not pursued. Propagates onto ~1870 → **~1900**.

- **4.4.1 = 4.4.0 + ≈ 15.** The **repetition fix** ([§ 12.23](roadmap.md)) — the entry whose number came from counting events rather than from the score. The search could not see a threefold repetition coming: it asked for three occurrences, declined at the second, and then took a transposition-table answer written before the position was a repetition, so the third was never reached at any depth. myChess therefore walked into draws from positions it considered won — one lichess game was drawn with it roughly eight pawns up. The number is open because the effect is **too small for a normal match to resolve**: at a plausible +5…+15 Elo it sits inside the ±14 Elo interval of a 1600-game run, and pinning it down would take five figures of games. The measurement that does work counts the event instead of the score — repetition draws split by which side believed it was winning, tested against the 50/50 that engine symmetry predicts (`tools/analyze-repetition-match.py`). That is exactly how it played out: the SPRT accepted H1 after 321 games at +42.4 ± 29.4, a number inflated by the early stop, while the event count came back **0 to 18** (p = 7.6 × 10⁻⁶) — in 321 games the corrected build never once walked into a repetition from a won position. Reading the asymmetry as points gives ≈ +10 Elo from that channel alone, which is why the column says ≈ +15 and not +42.

**Absolute uncertainty.** The propagated 95 %-CI on 4.0.0 is roughly `±31 (anchor) + ±20 (3.6.0) + ±15 (4.0.0)` ≈ **±66 Elo** under naive addition, or **±~40 Elo under independent-error addition** (sqrt of sum of squares). The 1562 estimate could be anywhere from ~1520 to ~1600 in CCRL-Blitz terms. The relative deltas between versions are far better-known than the absolute number.

**That re-anchor has now happened, and the propagated chain held.** On 2026-08-17 v4.4.1 was measured directly — 2000 games against five externally rated engines (TSCP 1609, Zeta Dva 1801, Princhess 1985, BBC 2019 fixed; Kojiro free), TC 40/120, combined with Ordo: **1928 ± 21**. The chain of self-play deltas had predicted **~1915**. Over roughly 500 Elo and a dozen versions the deltas transferred to external opponents almost one-for-one — which the ±40 propagated uncertainty above allowed for but did not promise, and which the literature warns against assuming. Full result, per-anchor breakdown and the two remaining deviations from CCRL conditions in [ELO measurement § 7](myChess-ELO-measurement.md#the-v441-re-anchor--measured-2026-08-17).

The propagated numbers for 3.2.0 through 4.4.0 stay as they are: they remain the best available estimates for *those* versions, and they are now known to be well-calibrated rather than merely plausible. The next re-anchor is due when the accumulated deltas since 4.4.1 exceed roughly the ±21 of this measurement — i.e. after another 30 to 50 Elo of shipped gains, not after every release.
